Oh man what a wild few weeks its been. Last time I posted I had just deployed my art revamp, and was working diligently on the conductor. Well after many hours and prompts I finally got it into the engine and working! The big downside to it - it was incredibly clunky and unusable! Instead of letting a bad UI/UX ruin my engine I decided to take some big swings. I broke out the composition half I had been working on and broke it out into it's own new app - Dr Maxwell's Midi-Evil! I was super inspired by Nanostudio (which is the only app I've ever successfully made a song on), so I figured it'd be cool to have a Rust app that could do the same for my game engine. As I had some agents building that out I also took some time to introduce more quality-of-life improvements into the Engine's editor. Added in some functionality to where you can now open rust scripts in the editor to your favorite external IDE - same goes for art (.png - opens to aseprite) and music (.midi - opens to midi-evil) files. Ideally I'm going to make it to where the engine can maybe just take in an .aseprite or .evil (the project file type for midi-evil) file and work with them natively, but for now getting the files opened and properly saved in the editor after edits is what I got in. On top of that I'm also getting the editor's panels to be a little easier to work with, and a little bit more structured in their presentation. Also the conductor has it's own clean panel now, and should let you trigger sound effects from your game to be in-sync with the midi music of your choice!

Up next on my plate is getting midi-evil web compatible and friendly then getting it deployed here for everyone to play with and make music on! After that I think I'll finally be able to actually focus on the core level design of the games I got deployed here. With art, music, and core programming finally starting to come together I can start dog-fooding this engine + editor to start building out levels, worlds, and stories. Nothing wrong with the classics, but where's the fun in releasing an unoriginal clone? Looking forward to showing off everything, and hopefully introducing people to some of the tools I've been creating to build it!
