My secret sauce.

I don’t want it to be a secret! People on the internet are always telling
you to pay them for the secret AI agenda system. The systems I have seen
are like my current one. It’s a long-winded and confusing questionnaire
that spits out something that proves to be, let’s say, less than useful.

My 14-day repeating system and my daily block system, as well as my
addition-for-subtraction method of IRL greenfield deployment, not to
mention my revolving time-and-location menu system. All of the work that I
put into making each of those reusable and repeatable systems, has been
awesome. It has helped me juggle, what seemed to be easy for everyone else.

I realize now that most people struggle with getting it all done or
keeping a routine. The number of hats a person wears in a day can become
unmanageable for even the most talented; No man is an island.

Watching Jesse make a profile tonight for FortKnight was rough but
informative. I found a lot of bugs and had some ideas for new features but
having to re-explain and have him rearrange his goals because he had input
them the opposite of how I had designed them made me squirm.

Enoughism is hard.

— M
