My Secret Sauce

I don’t want it to be a secret! People on the internet are always telling you to pay them for the secret AI agenda system. The systems I have seen are like my current one. It’s a long-winded and confusing questionnaire that spits out something that proves to be, let’s say, less than useful.

My 14-day repeating system and my daily block system, plus my addition-for-subtraction method of IRL greenfield deployment, not to mention my revolving time-and-location menu system. All the work I put into making each of those reusable and repeatable systems has been awesome. It has helped me juggle what seemed easy for everyone else.

I realize now that most people struggle to get it all done or keep a routine. The number of hats a person wears in a day can become unmanageable for even the most talented; No man is an island.

Watching Jesse make a profile tonight for Fort Knight was rough but informative. I found a lot of bugs and had some ideas for new features. Having to re-explain and have him rearrange his goals because he entered them the opposite way I designed them made me squirm.

Jesse has taught me a lot about code and basically everything I know about best practices and structure, so putting something unfinished in front of him, not to mention the world at large, is pretty cringe. I'm excited to continue to create and learn

— M
