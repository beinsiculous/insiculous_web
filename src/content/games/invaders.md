The Toasted are marching down the counter: four rows of haunted bread — the
two-hit Sour-Doom, Bagelgeist (fire through the hole in its ring and the shot
sails on), Rye.I.P. and Plain Wraith. Deion holds the line, firing icicles from
his mohawk, behind four butter bunkers that chip into holes and melt as they
take damage. Bust the last slice to win.

Maxwell crosses the top lane on his cake saucer for a mystery bonus — and now
and then it is **Jammer** instead, dropping jam that jams your shot for a
moment.

Co-op is the real draw: Cubert joins as a second cannon firing ice chips, with
his own lives and score, defending the same bunkers against the same
descending fleet. In **Ridiculous** and **Insiculous** each cannon fires a twin
volley. Eleven achievements, chaos modes included.

Built on Insiculous 2D. Playable right here in the browser (WebGPU —
Chrome/Edge, or Firefox with `dom.webgpu.enabled`); desktop builds
(Vulkan / Metal / DX12) run the same code natively.
