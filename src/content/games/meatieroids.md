Deion pilots the **Flying Funk** — a gravy boat seen from above — through a
belt of **meatieroids**: roided-out meatballs that tumble, bounce off each
other, and flex at you. Fire icicles to bust them. A large one bursts into
two medium ones, a medium into two small, and the children come out flexing,
fanned out and faster than their parent. The small ones are the hardest to
hit, so they pay the most.

Thrust, turn, and drift: the Funk turns smoothly, and its thrust and its shots
follow the heading drawn. A lost ship plays the Funk's crash, and the respawn
blinks invincible for a moment. Extra ships arrive every 10,000 points, and
the run only ends when the ships do.

Co-op shares one belt: Cubert flies a second Funk firing ice chips, with his
own lives and score. In **Ridiculous** and **Insiculous** a large meatieroid
bursts into three. Eleven achievements, chaos modes included.

Built on Insiculous 2D. Playable right here in the browser (WebGPU —
Chrome/Edge, or Firefox with `dom.webgpu.enabled`); desktop builds
(Vulkan / Metal / DX12) run the same code natively.
